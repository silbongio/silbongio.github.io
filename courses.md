I primarily teach courses in Spanish phonetics and phonology, both at the undergraduate and graduate levels. My teaching emphasizes the connection between linguistic theory and real-world language use, and I aim to create inclusive, engaging classroom environments that support student inquiry and development. I also enjoy mentoring students on research projects related to second language acquisition and pronunciation.

Below, you’ll find brief descriptions of the courses I teach, along with selected materials—click on the course titles to learn more.

My teaching extends beyond the classroom as well. I mentor undergraduate students through the <a href="https://excelnetwork.cal.msu.edu/undergraduate-research/" target="_blank">College of Arts and Letters' Undergraduate Research Initiative (URI)</a>, supporting their development as independent researchers. I also co-direct PhonoGroup, a weekly phonology lab jointly run with colleagues in Linguistics (Dr. Scott Borgeson, Dr. Karthik Durvasula and Dr. Yen-Hwei Lin), which brings together graduate students and faculty for discussion of ongoing research in phonology and phonetics.
