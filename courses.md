I primarily teach courses in Spanish phonetics and phonology, both at the undergraduate and graduate levels. My teaching emphasizes the connection between linguistic theory and real-world language use, and I aim to create inclusive, engaging classroom environments that support student inquiry and development. I also enjoy mentoring students on research projects related to second language acquisition and pronunciation.

My teaching extends beyond the classroom as well. I mentor undergraduate students through the <a href="https://excelnetwork.cal.msu.edu/undergraduate-research/" target="_blank">College of Arts and Letters' Undergraduate Research Initiative (CAL-URI)</a>, supporting their development as independent researchers. I also co-direct <a href= "https://karthikdurvasula.gitlab.io/MSUPhonoGroupAndEMALab.html" target="_blank"> PhonoGroup</a>, a weekly phonology lab jointly run with colleagues in Linguistics (<a href="https://borgeson-linguist.com/" target="_blank">Dr. Scott Borgeson</a>, <a href="https://karthikdurvasula.gitlab.io/" target="_blank">Dr. Karthik Durvasula</a> and <a href="https://liny.msu.domains/" target="_blank">Dr. Yen-Hwei Lin</a>), which brings together graduate students and faculty for discussion of ongoing research in phonology and phonetics.
