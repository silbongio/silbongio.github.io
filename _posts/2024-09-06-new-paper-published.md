title: "New Paper Published in Studies in Hispanic and Lusophone Linguistics"
---

My article, <a href="https://doi.org/10.1515/shll-2024-2009" target="_blank"> "Accounting for effects of monitored speech: vowel nasalization, nasal consonant weakening and /s/ production in Spanish,"</a> has been published in Studies in Hispanic and Lusophone Linguistics. The paper explores how speakers of Dominican and Argentine Spanish adjust their pronunciation under formal speech conditions, using /s/ production as a reference point to probe /n/ weakening and anticipatory vowel nasalization.
