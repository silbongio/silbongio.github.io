My paper with <a href="https://betsysneller.github.io/">Betsy Sneller</a> and <a href="https://scholars.msu.edu/scholar/9275/CHANTAL-TETREAULT?unitId=665&unitType=2">Chantal Tetreault </a>. "Commentary - Exploring Stylistic Repertoires Across the Lifespan" is out! This paper is a chapter in Isabelle Buchstaller and Karen Beaman's edited volume <i><a href="https://www.routledge.com/Connecting-the-Individual-and-the-Community-in-Sociolinguistic-Panel-Research/Buchstaller-Beaman/p/book/9781032413082">Connecting the Individual and the Community in Sociolinguistic Panel Research</i></a>.

Our chapter comments on two papers examining stylistic variation across the lifespan of two public figures: David Attenborough and Michaëlle Jean. The two papers – by Laurel MacKenzie, and by Josiane Riverin-Coutlée and Jonathan Harrington – illustrate innovative and robust ways to push forward a sociolinguistics of change over the lifespan that effectively accounts for style. And, each, in their own way, provides cutting-edge, robust, and meticulous scholarship about a highly complex topic and remarkably conclusive evidence for the relative stability of speaker style over the lifespan.
