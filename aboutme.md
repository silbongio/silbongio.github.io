I was born and raised in Buenos Aires, Argentina. I completed my undergraduate degree at the <a href="http://www.filo.uba.ar">Universidad de Buenos Aires (Facultad de Filosofía y Letras)</a>, where I studied <i>Letras</i>. I showed up my very first day ready to study literature, but had one class in Linguistics and the rest is history. 

I earned a dual-PhD in <a href="http://www.indiana.edu/~lingdept/">Linguistics </a> and <a href="http://www.indiana.edu/~spanport/" target="_blank">Hispanic Linguistics</a> at Indiana University,under the supervision of Prof. <a href="http://cl.indiana.edu/~kdejong/">Kenneth de Jong </a> and Prof. <a href="https://indiana.academia.edu/ErikWillis">Erik W. Willis</a>. My main area of research is experimental phonology: I study the phonetic underpinnings of phonological variation, with a focus on Spanish. I have also published work in sociolinguistics and second language acquisition.

I am currently Associate Professor of Spanish, in the <a href="https://rcs.msu.edu/">Department of Romance and Classical Studies</a> at Michigan State University. I am also adjunct faculty in the Linguistics program, core faculty in the Center of Latin American Studies and affiliated in Second Language Studies and Digital Humanities. So many links! But now you know: cool people all around me!
