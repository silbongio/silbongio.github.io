##### Course description

<p>This course presents an overview of grammatical structures of Spanish. It is intended to introduce students to linguistic descriptions of Spanish and guide them as they develop metalinguistic knowledge of Spanish. Students will be introduced to core linguistic concepts and analytical tools to examine word and sentence structure.</p>
<br>

##### Learning goales

<p>By the end of this course, students will:
  <ul>
    <li>Develop a foundational understanding of the Spanish sound system.</li>
    <li>Understand and apply key concepts in phonetics and phonology to the analysis of Spanish.</li>
    <li>Gain familiarity with major theoretical frameworks in phonological research.</li>
    <li>Explore dialectal variation in Spanish and its phonological implications.</li>
    <li>Strengthen academic skills through research, analysis, and scholarly writing.</li>
</ul></p>
<br>

##### Learning outcomes

<p>By the end of the course, students will be able to:
<ul>
<li>Describe the articulatory and acoustic properties of Spanish sounds.</li>
<li>Explain fundamental principles of phonological analysis and apply them to Spanish data.</li>
<li>Analyze Spanish phonological processes using transcription, data sets, and theoretical tools.</li>
<li>Compare key theoretical approaches to phonology (e.g., Optimality Theory, Exemplar Theory) and assess how they account for Spanish data.</li>
<li>Identify and discuss patterns of dialectal variation in Spanish phonetics and phonology.</li>
<li>Synthesize course concepts in written assignments and a final research paper. </li>
</ul></p>
